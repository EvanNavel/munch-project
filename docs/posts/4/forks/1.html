Turkey Lettuce Wrap Tacos

2 Sweets
  • Forked by: alice.smith@example.com
  • Original Author: david.brown@example.com
  • Meal: Dinner
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Cuisine: Mexican
  • Dietary: Low Carb

Description

A lighter take on the beef tacos, swapping tortillas for crisp lettuce cups.

Ingredients

1 lb ground turkey 1 packet taco seasoning 1 head butter lettuce 1 cup pico de gallo 1 avocado, sliced

Directions

Brown the turkey and stir in the seasoning with a splash of water. Spoon into lettuce cups. Top with pico de gallo and avocado.
