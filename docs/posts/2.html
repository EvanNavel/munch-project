Chicken Tikka Masala

3 Sweets
  • Author: bob.johnson@example.com
  • Meal: Dinner
  • Difficulty: Hard
  • Cuisine: Indian
  • Dietary: Gluten Free

Description

A popular Indian dish with a creamy tomato sauce.

Ingredients

- 500g boneless chicken, cut into pieces - 1 cup yogurt - 1 tablespoon lemon juice - 2 teaspoons cumin - 1 teaspoon cinnamon - 2 teaspoons cayenne pepper - 2 teaspoons black pepper - 1 tablespoon ginger - 1 teaspoon salt - 3 cloves garlic, minced - 1 onion, diced - 2 cups tomato sauce - 2 cups cream

Directions

1. Marinate the chicken in the yogurt, lemon juice, and spices for an hour. 2. Saute the garlic and onions in a pan, then add the tomato sauce and cream. 3. Add the chicken and simmer for 20 minutes.
