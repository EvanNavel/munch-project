Grandma's Apple Pie

5 Sweets
  • Author: alice.smith@example.com
  • Meal: Snack
  • Difficulty: Medium
  • Cuisine: American
  • Dietary: Gluten Free

Description

A classic dessert with a sweet and tangy filling.

Ingredients

- 1 pie crust - 6 cups of peeled and sliced apples - 1 cup of sugar - 2 tablespoons of all-purpose flour - 1/2 teaspoon of ground cinnamon - 1/4 teaspoon of ground nutmeg - 1 tablespoon of lemon juice

Directions

1. Preheat your oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C). 2. Place the apple slices into the pie crust. 3. Mix the sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice in a bowl, then pour over the apples. 4. Bake for about 50 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown.
